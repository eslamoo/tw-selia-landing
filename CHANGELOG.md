# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-26)

First release.

- Eleven sections from the Selia theme: page header with the store logo, animated text bar, advanced slider with image and video slides,
  product card with quick buy, animated payment methods, features, double banner, three banners,
  images grid, customer reviews and FAQ.
- Two landing page templates, each built around one product: "abaya-elegance" (light) and
  "perfume-signature" (dark).
- Calls to action open the landing page's fast checkout on product pages and follow the page's main
  link on promotion pages. The quantity chosen in the product card is passed to the checkout.
- Swipeable product images (touch and mouse drag) synced with the thumbnails.
- A light/dark switch in the header that changes the whole page and remembers the shopper's choice, with a
  default mode per page and a per-section override.
- Transparent header over the slider and a sticky header, as in the Selia theme.
- The animated text bar and advanced slider match Selia's: speed, sizes, caption, button and overlay.
- The product card's marquee moves at Selia's speed, with Selia's spacing under the thumbnails.
- Animated payment methods use Selia's animated brands cards, with a reversed second row.
- The sticky header turns frosted (blurred) while scrolling, as in Selia.
- Both templates use every section, with double and triple banners and an image grid designed for each
  template's product in Arabic and English.
- Banners stack correctly on phones.
- Arabic and English text for every merchant field, store colors and font, phone layouts.
