# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-26)

First release.

- Eleven sections from the Selia theme: page header with the store logo, animated text bar, advanced slider with image and video slides,
  product card with quick buy, animated payment methods, features, double banner, three banners,
  images grid, customer reviews and FAQ.
- Two landing page templates: "abaya-elegance" for product pages and "fashion-offers" for promotion
  pages.
- Calls to action open the landing page's fast checkout on product pages and follow the page's main
  link on promotion pages. The quantity chosen in the product card is passed to the checkout.
- Swipeable product images (touch and mouse drag) synced with the thumbnails.
- Light, dark and automatic color schemes per section; the offers template ships in dark.
- Arabic and English text for every merchant field, store colors and font, phone layouts.
